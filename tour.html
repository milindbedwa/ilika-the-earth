    About this model

    From the drawings

    Room positions, sizes, doors, glazing, the pool, the sunken sitting area, both terraces, the outside stair and the furniture layout are taken from the ground and first floor plans, at the scale given by the dimension schedule. The plot, gate, trees and garden pavilion follow the master plan.

    From the renders

    Terracotta lime plaster, the jaali crown over the multi room, brick jaali screens at the entrance, the planted pergolas, the urns and the late afternoon light.

    Design proposal

    Interiors are not shown in the drawings. Finishes, furniture, lighting, bathroom fittings, ceiling heights (10 ft on the ground floor), the 2 ft plinth and all planting species are our proposal in the spirit of the renders.

    Still open

    • The mud bath is labelled 5′0″ × 32″ but drawn about 10′0″ × 6′3″. It is modelled as drawn.
    • Outdoor sitting is given as 20′ × 30″ running ft. It is modelled as a built-in seat 30″ deep along two parapets.
    • The outside stair appears on both plans without risers or a landing size. It is modelled as a straight flight but the tour changes floor with a fade.
    • The pool render shows the upper volume about 38 ft wide; the first floor plan gives a 22′9″ room plus bathroom. The room follows the plan and the jaali crown overhangs it.
    • Both terraces are labelled 21′6″ × 36′6″, yet they are L-shaped on plan. They are modelled as the roof of the floor below.
    • The pool on the first floor plan is the ground floor pool seen from above. There is one pool.
