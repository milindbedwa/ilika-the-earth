02 Lime plaster · चूना

Why lime plaster?

Naturally regulates temperature, keeping interiors cooler and more comfortable. A premium, labour-intensive technique that adds lasting character and luxury.

Benefits of
lime plaster

We chose traditional lime plaster as a natural alternative to cement-based finishes — breathable, durable, and made to age beautifully with the architecture.

  • Signature lime plaster finish

    A premium architectural finish that adds depth, warmth, and enduring luxury to every surface.

  • Thermal comfort

    Breathable walls for cooler, more comfortable interiors — reducing indoor temperatures by up to 6–8°C.

  • Lower environmental impact

    Made from natural mineral materials with a lower environmental footprint than many conventional finishes.

  • Resists bacteria, mould & mildew

    Being alkaline, it does not allow the growth of mould, mildew and some bacteria.

  • Artisanal & handcrafted

    Handcrafted lime plaster with refined, timeless character where natural texture meets sophisticated elegance.

  • Repairable

    Unlike many modern finishes, lime plaster can be maintained and repaired without replacing the entire surface.

  • Breathable

    Allows walls to naturally regulate moisture and indoor humidity.

  • UV safe

    Lime plaster is typically 1,500 to 2,000 microns thick — a mineral finish made to last for generations.

  • Purifies air

    Hypoallergenic and good for indoor air quality — kinder for people with breathing problems.

  • Ages gracefully

    Its subtle texture and natural patina become part of the building’s character over time.

  • Naturally durable

    Lime finishes develop character over time rather than looking worn or artificial.

  • Preserving tradition

    Specialised craftsmanship ensures lasting beauty while protecting the integrity of the lime plaster finish.

05 Sustainable design

Why sustainable design?

Sustainability is not just a design choice; it is a way of living in harmony with nature. Through climate-responsive architecture, natural materials, lime plaster finishes and thoughtfully curated spaces, we create homes that breathe naturally, connect you with the outdoors and promote everyday well-being.

Climate responsive design

A farmhouse designed around the rhythm of nature.

  • Architecture in harmony

    Design that responds to sun, wind, shade and seasonal rhythms to create naturally comfortable spaces.

  • Passive cooling, naturally

    Thoughtful orientation, shaded openings and cross ventilation help reduce dependence on mechanical cooling.

  • Shade by design

    Deep overhangs, verandahs, pergolas and landscaped buffers create cooler transitions between indoors and outdoors.

  • Cross ventilation

    Aligned openings encourage natural air movement, keeping living spaces fresh and comfortable.

  • Landscape as a climate element

    Trees, planting and green buffers are thoughtfully integrated to provide shade, reduce heat and soften the microclimate.

  • The luxury of natural comfort

    A farmhouse designed not just to look beautiful, but to feel naturally cooler, brighter and more connected to the outdoors.
